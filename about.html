Who we are

A Malaysian company shaped around dependable public-sector delivery.

Ikram Test Sdn. Bhd. is a 100% Bumiputera-owned company incorporated in 2007 and officially registered with the Ministry of Finance Malaysia.

Our experience spans government vehicles, uniforms and textiles, safety equipment, heavy machinery and specialised logistics for ministries, municipalities, enforcement agencies and statutory bodies nationwide.

Our mission

Execute every requirement with precision and accountability.

  1. 01

    Deliver compliant, timely and cost-effective supply solutions to ministries, GLCs and government agencies.

  2. 02

    Provide end-to-end execution from tendering and sourcing through modification, delivery and training.

  3. 03

    Build long-term client relationships through reliability, transparency and performance.

  4. 04

    Support Bumiputera enterprise growth and sustainable economic contribution.

  5. 05

    Maintain strict compliance with MOF procurement policies and quality standards.

Our vision

To be the nation’s most reliable, efficient and respected Bumiputera partner in logistics, procurement and public-sector supply chains.
